# Concerned Citizens Witzenberg Website

Official website of **Concerned Citizens Witzenberg** – a paper-free, community-driven platform for civic action, road safety, and sustainable development.

## Overview

**Concerned Citizens Witzenberg** aims to bring local communities together to address important civic issues, promote road safety awareness, and drive sustainable development initiatives. This platform will be eco-friendly, focusing on digital tools to raise awareness and resolve issues within the community.

## Features

- **Community-driven**: A space where citizens can raise and resolve local issues.
- **Road Safety**: Highlighting important road safety concerns and initiatives.
- **Sustainable Development**: Promoting eco-friendly practices and sustainable growth within the community.
- **Paper-free**: Operates with a strong focus on digital solutions to minimize paper waste.

- CSS3
- JavaScript (Optional, for future enhancements)
